# Product Decisions

## Mobile launch
The mobile launch was postponed after the September pilot. The team found that 78% of sessions happened on desktop and the mobile ingestion flow failed on large PDFs. Engineering capacity moved to source citations and upload reliability.

## Pricing
We chose a free tier with 100 memory chunks, a $49 monthly Founder plan, and custom enterprise pricing. The team will revisit usage limits after the first 20 paying customers.
